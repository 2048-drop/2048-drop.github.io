How to play 2048 Drop

A numbered block falls from the top of a five-column well. Use the left and right arrow keys to steer it while it falls, the down arrow to speed it up, and the up arrow or spacebar to slam it straight down. When the block lands next to another block holding the same number — above, below, or to either side — the two fuse into a single block worth double.

Every merge collapses the stack, which can push new pairs together and set off a chain reaction. Longer chains are worth more: a merge in the third link of a cascade scores triple. Clearing space is the whole game, because when the well fills to the top it is over.

Tips for a higher score

  • Keep your largest block in a bottom corner and build a descending staircase toward it.
  • Do not scatter 2s across all five columns — park them next to each other so they collapse on contact.
  • Use the hard drop only when you are certain; a misplaced block in the middle of the well is hard to dig out.
  • Chains beat single merges. Setting up a column so one landing triggers three merges is worth far more than three separate landings.

About the game

2048 Drop crosses the falling-block puzzle format with the merge maths of 2048. It runs entirely in your browser, works offline once loaded, needs no account or download, and plays on desktop and mobile. Your best score is stored locally on your own device.
