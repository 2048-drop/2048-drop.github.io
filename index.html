A falling-block puzzle with 2048 maths

2048 Drop takes the well and the falling pieces of a classic block-drop game and replaces line clearing with the merge arithmetic of 2048. A numbered block descends through a five-column well. Steer it left and right, and when you are ready, send it straight to the floor. If it comes to rest touching another block of the same number, the two fuse into one block of double the value.

That single rule is what makes the game interesting, because merges leave holes. Everything above a merged pair falls into the gap, which can push two more matching blocks together, which merges those, which opens another gap. A well-placed 2 can set off a chain that clears half the board. Chains pay a multiplier: the third link of a cascade is worth three times its face value, so the difference between a tidy board and a scoring board is knowing where the dominoes are.

What is in the game

Three speeds

Chill for planning, Classic for the standard curve, Blitz when you want the well to fill faster than you can think.

Daily Challenge

Every player gets the same seeded sequence of blocks each day. Same pieces, same order — the only variable is how you place them.

Power-ups

Bombs clear a three-by-three hole. Wild blocks take the value of their strongest neighbour and guarantee a merge. Undo takes back a bad drop.

Hold slot

Bank the current block and bring it back when it is useful. The classic escape hatch for an awkward piece.

Landing preview

A dashed outline shows exactly where the block will come to rest, and turns green when that landing will trigger a merge.

16 achievements

From your first fusion to a five-link avalanche, with statistics and a personal top-ten table kept on your own device.

Controls

  • Left and right arrows — steer the falling block between columns.
  • Down arrow (or Up, or Space) — drop it straight to the floor. Distance travelled scores two points a row.
  • C — hold the current block, or swap it with whatever is already held.
  • 1, 2, 3 — bomb, wild block, undo.
  • P pause, R restart.
  • On a phone — drag left and right to steer, tap to drop, swipe up to hold.

Where the points come from

Every merge scores the value of the block it creates, multiplied by its position in the chain. A 64 formed on the first link is worth 64; the same 64 formed on the third link of a cascade is worth 192. Hard drops add two points per row travelled, which is small but adds up across a long game. Every 2,500 points hands you a random power-up.

The well fills one level faster every 1,500 points in Classic. There is no win condition and no ending — 2048 is a milestone, not a finish line, and the board keeps going as long as you can keep the top row clear.
