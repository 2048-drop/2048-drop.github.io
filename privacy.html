Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026

This policy explains what information 2048 Drop (“the Site”, at https://avalonlover1.github.io/2048-drop) collects, why, and what you can do about it. It is written to be read, not to be skimmed past.

The short version

  • There is no account, no login, and no sign-up form. We never ask for your name or email.
  • Your scores, statistics and settings are saved in your own browser and never sent to us.
  • We show ads through Google AdSense. Google, not us, may set cookies and use them to choose which ads you see.
  • You can play the entire game with non-personalised ads, or with an ad blocker, and nothing breaks.

Information stored on your device

The Site uses your browser’s local storage — not a server — to remember:

  • your best score, top-ten score table, and lifetime statistics;
  • which achievements you have unlocked;
  • your chosen theme, sound setting and difficulty mode;
  • your advertising consent choice.

This data never leaves your device and we cannot read it. It is not tied to any identifier. Clearing your browser’s site data, or using the Reset all data button in the Statistics panel, erases it permanently. Because it is stored per-browser, your scores will not follow you to another device.

Information collected automatically

The Site itself runs no analytics and keeps no server logs of its own. Our host, GitHub Pages, may record standard technical request data such as IP address, browser type and requested page as part of delivering the site and protecting it from abuse. That is governed by the host’s own privacy policy.

Advertising

We use Google AdSense, including its rewarded-video format, to fund the Site.

  • Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.
  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info/choices or the NAI opt-out page.
  • Google’s own explanation of how it uses data from sites that use its services is at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Third-party ad networks other than Google may also serve ads through Google’s ad exchange. We do not control their cookies, and their privacy practices are their own.

Rewarded video

Some optional rewards in the game — a second chance after a game over, a power-up refill — are offered in exchange for watching a short video advertisement. Watching is always optional, always initiated by you pressing a button, and declining costs you nothing but the reward. We never ask you to click an advertisement, and no reward in this game is ever given for clicking one.

Your choices

Consent banner

On your first visit you are asked whether to accept personalised advertising or to receive non-personalised ads instead. Choosing non-personalised means Google is instructed not to use your browsing history to select ads; you will still see ads, chosen by context rather than profile. You can change this at any time from Settings → Change ad consent in the game.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland (GDPR)

Where we or Google rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time using the link above. You have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, and to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority. Because we hold no personal data ourselves — everything the game stores is on your own device — most of these requests are exercised directly against Google, or satisfied by clearing your browser data. Contact us at [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL] if you need help.

If you are in California (CCPA/CPRA)

We do not sell or share personal information in the ordinary sense of those words, and we receive no money from any transfer of personal information. Advertising cookies set by Google may nonetheless constitute “sharing for cross-context behavioural advertising” under California law. To opt out, choose Non-personalised in the consent banner or in Settings, and use the Google Ads Settings link above. California residents have the right to know, delete, correct, and to be free from discrimination for exercising those rights.

Children

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us at [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL] and we will act on it. Parents and guardians should be aware that advertisements shown on this Site are served by third parties and are not reviewed by us in advance.

Security

The Site is served over HTTPS. It has no user accounts, no database and no forms, so there are no credentials to steal from us. Data saved by the game is only as secure as the device it is saved on.

External links

Advertisements and some links lead to sites we do not operate. We are not responsible for their content or their privacy practices. Read their policies before giving them anything.

Changes to this policy
